This page explains what happens with your data when you visit GrowthLane.

## Cookies and analytics

GrowthLane uses Google Analytics to see which articles are read. It sets
cookies only if you press **Accept** on the cookie notice. If you press
**Decline**, or ignore the notice, no analytics script is loaded and no
analytics cookies are set. Your choice is saved in your own browser
(local storage on your device) so the notice does not keep reappearing, and
you can change it at any time with the **Cookie settings** link in the
footer. Declining removes the Google Analytics cookies from this site.

When analytics is on, Google receives usage information such as the pages you
view, your approximate location and your browser and device type, as described
in [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Affiliate partners that this site links to (Amazon, ClickBank, Digistore24 and
similar) may set their own cookies after you click an affiliate link, in order
to track referrals. I don't control those cookies; see each partner's own
privacy policy for details.

## What I collect

GrowthLane does not collect personal information directly: there are no
accounts, comments or forms. The only data processed is the analytics data
above, and only if you accept. If that changes in the future (for example,
adding an email newsletter), this page will be updated and any signup will
require your explicit, separate consent.

## Third-party links

This site contains affiliate links to third-party merchants. Once you leave
this site, their own privacy policy applies, not this one.

## Your rights

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have rights under GDPR regarding any personal
data processed about you, including access, correction, and deletion.
You can withdraw your analytics consent at any time with the Cookie settings
link in the footer. Because this site does not directly collect personal data
beyond optional analytics, there is usually nothing further to request from me;
if you have a question, write to the address below.
