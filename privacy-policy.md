# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: {{ site.time | date: "%B %Y" }}_

This page explains what happens with your data when you visit GrowthLane.

## Cookies

This site may use cookies for basic analytics (to understand which articles
are useful) and third parties we link to (ClickBank, Digistore24, and
similar affiliate partners) may set their own cookies when you click an
affiliate link, in order to track referrals. We don't control those
third-party cookies — see the relevant partner's own privacy policy for
details.

## What we collect

GrowthLane itself does not currently collect any personal information
directly (no account creation, no forms). If that changes in the future
(for example, adding an email newsletter), this page will be updated and
any signup will require your explicit, separate consent.

## Third-party links

This site contains affiliate links to third-party merchants. Once you leave
this site, their own privacy policy applies, not this one.

## Your rights

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have rights under GDPR regarding any personal
data processed about you, including access, correction, and deletion.
Since this site does not directly collect personal data at this time, there
is currently nothing to request — this will be updated if that changes.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the [About]({{ '/about/' | relative_url }}) page.
