GrowthLane covers books, courses and tools for communication, sales, mindset and productivity. These are the rules every article is held to.

## Every article is labelled by how I know it

<span class="chip chip--first">First-hand</span> means I read or used the product myself. The opinions and ratings in the article are mine.

<span class="chip chip--research">Research-based</span> means I did not use it personally. The article is built from public sources such as publisher pages, author biographies and independent reviews, and it says what I could not verify.

I would rather label a review as research-based than pretend to an experience I did not have.

## What I look for before recommending anything

- A named, real author or company with a background you can check.
- Independent reviews, not only affiliate blogs, and an honest look at complaints and refund problems.
- A clear price and a real guarantee.
- No fake urgency, anonymous authors, unverifiable testimonials or income promises. Products built on those are skipped entirely.

## Downsides, every time

Each recommendation says who it is best for and where it falls short. If a review has no downsides, it is an advert.

## How affiliate links fit in

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you buy through one, I may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. That never changes the verdict, and a product with a commission link can still get a lukewarm review. The full text is on the [affiliate disclosure]({{ '/disclosure/' | relative_url }}) page.

## How the articles are made

Articles are researched and drafted with AI assistance, then checked and approved by me before they are published. Personal opinions and ratings are only ever mine, which is why first-hand articles are marked as such.

Every article shows when it was published and when it was last updated.
