## The short version

Dr. Joe Rubino's **Life Optimization Coaching Certification Program** is a self-paced audio-and-video course built around six areas of life (health, money, work, relationships, personal growth, and recreation), with an optional exam if you want to use it to coach others professionally. It costs **$97**, comes with a **60-day money-back guarantee**, and there's a separate **$97 upsell** offered after checkout that you do not need to buy to get the core program.

Who it's genuinely good for: someone who wants a structured way to work through personal goals instead of a scattered pile of advice, or someone seriously considering coaching as a second career and wants a low-cost way to test the waters.

Who should skip it: anyone expecting the certification itself to open doors the way a university degree or an accredited license would — more on that below.

## Who's actually behind this

Dr. Joe Rubino has been coaching for over 30 years, has personally worked with more than 2,000 clients, and has books translated into 24 languages. He's the CEO of the Center for Personal Reinvention, a real, findable business. One of the endorsements on his sales page comes from Bob Burg, known for the business book *The Go-Giver* — a genuine name in that space.

To be fair about the limits of what we can confirm from the outside: Rubino's own site doesn't show recent blog posts or dated activity, so it reads more like a long-running, steady operation than an actively buzzing one. That's not automatically a bad sign for a course built around timeless principles — but it's honest to say it up front rather than pretend otherwise.

## What you actually get

- **27 audio recordings** covering the core coaching method
- **26 written transcripts** of that same content, if you'd rather read than listen
- **4 video trainings** — building a life-optimization plan, setting up a personal-development structure, finding your purpose, and understanding what coaching actually does
- An **optional 50-question certification exam** if you want the credential — per the vendor's own sales page, you need 70% correct to pass

One thing worth being precise about: it's genuinely unclear from the sales page who formally recognizes this "certification," or whether it carries weight with clients or employers the way an accredited credential would. If your main reason for buying is the certificate itself rather than the material, that's a question worth emailing the vendor about *before* you pay, not after.

## What's genuinely good about it

The six-area structure (health, wealth, work, relationships, personal growth, recreation) is a sensible way to organize personal development instead of treating "get better" as one vague blob. The content also focuses on practical mechanics — identifying limiting beliefs, making requests that actually get followed through on, building presence rather than performance — rather than abstract motivational language.

The most refreshing part, honestly, is what the sales page *doesn't* claim. Right on the page, Rubino states plainly that **most people who buy any informational program never build a business from it** — he's not pretending this is a guaranteed income stream. That kind of self-imposed honesty is rare enough in this space that it's worth pointing out.

## The real downsides

- **The "certification" itself is loosely defined.** As above — don't buy this expecting a formally accredited coaching license.
- **It's broad by design.** Six life areas in one program means less depth per area than a course focused on just one thing (say, only public speaking, or only budgeting). If you want deep, narrow expertise, this isn't that.
- **$97 plus a $97 upsell can add up**, even though the upsell is optional. Read the checkout page carefully so you know what you're saying yes to.
- **No recent, dated case studies or updates** on the vendor's own site, which makes it harder to judge how the program has evolved recently versus a few years ago.

## Our take

If you're looking for a structured, one-time-purchase way to work through personal goals across the major areas of your life, and you go in treating the "certification" as a nice-to-have rather than the main event, this holds up reasonably well — largely because of who's behind it. If you're specifically shopping for a recognized professional coaching credential, this probably isn't that, and it's worth looking elsewhere or asking the vendor directly before you buy.
