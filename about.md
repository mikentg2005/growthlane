GrowthLane is a small, independent site about the skills that move a career and a life forward: communication, sales, mindset and the habits behind them.

I'm Mike. I live in Cyprus and I'm studying economics and finance. I read a lot of books and courses in this space, and I want a place that says plainly who each one is for, who should skip it, and where it falls short.

Most reviews of self-improvement products are either adverts in disguise or too vague to help you decide before you spend money. This site tries to do the opposite. When I have read or used something myself, the article says so. When I haven't, it says that too and sticks to what public sources show. You can see exactly how that works on the [How I review]({{ '/how-i-review/' | relative_url }}) page.

## How the site makes money

Some links on this site are affiliate links, meaning I may earn a small commission if you buy through them, at no extra cost to you. It never changes what I think of something. The full details are in the [affiliate disclosure]({{ '/disclosure/' | relative_url }}).
