GrowthLane is a small, independent site about the skills that actually move
a career and a life forward: communication, sales, leadership, and the
day-to-day habits behind them.

I read a lot of books and courses in this space, and I write about the ones
that seem genuinely useful — pointing out what's solid and what isn't,
instead of pretending everything is life-changing.
